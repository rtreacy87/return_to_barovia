# Vardak
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Where:** with me · **Toward us:** hostile; under my control

## What I know
The vampire sorcerer who held Ravenloft for [[karstark]]. (Iolas heard "Verik Morgain"; Kethri and I heard Vardak. The man himself was in no condition to correct us.) At Ravenloft I drew the circles and **banished the intelligent part of him**: the mind, the memory, the cunning. What remains in the body is the **negative-energy spirit** that animated it, and it *hates* the material plane. It is quiet, obedient and miserable, which suits me well enough for the present.

Naturally, the miserable thing is the fix. I need only find a few hours to **swap it for one of my positive-energy spirits**, and I have been looking forward to it for days. His **staff** is still unidentified.
