# The Vargulf
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Where:** Castle Ravenloft · **Toward us:** hostile (dead)

## What I know
A long-limbed, unnaturally tall vampiric cat, led by the sorcerer [[vardak]]. It left the deep claw marks Kethri found in the dungeon, killed [[dagomir]] from the rafters, and was trapped by Gwen and me inside a wall of force with sickening green radiance, then beheaded by Ismark's Vorpal Sword.
