# Madam Eva
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Where:** [[tser-pool]] · **Toward us:** ally, bound and wounded

## Who she is
A **Vistani seer**, whose caravan stood at the Tser Pool until twelve days before we arrived. What we met was not Eva as she was but what is left of her: a **banshee**, a wailing old woman's voice bound to the water by the witch **[[verona]]**, who stole her life. Of all the Vistani, she is the one who seems to have understood what was coming.

Gwen recovered Eva's **crystal ball** from the burned wreckage of her caravan, and carries it still. The guards of Vallaki regard it with suspicion.

## What she told us (Session 5, at the pool, through Iolas and Gwen)
- The vampire lords serve a **greater power** that seeks to **break Barovia's barrier** and pass into the realms beyond.
- They are searching for something in the **libraries of Vallaki**, something **hidden long ago in the crypts of Ravenloft**. (Hence [[quest-access-the-vallaki-library]] and [[quest-the-artifact]].)
- She warned us about **[[aenar]]**: *"Do not trust him."* She thinks he may have helped release the ancient power the witch serves.
- A **knight** came to the Vistani before the camp was raided, promising them *happiness and joy*. Eva could not sense whether the promise was true, so she **stayed**. She also said that her **purpose lies here**.
- Verona came with the three brothers ([[karstark]], [[erabus]], [[usheron]]), from whatever realm they came from. We got no other leads on her whereabouts.
- The Vistani left twelve days before our arrival, heading west.

After speaking she dissipated and the party returned to camp.

## What I make of her
She chose to stay when the others left, and she chose it on the strength of a feeling she could not verify. Staying was either an act of great courage or of great misjudgement, and in the end it cost her her life. I do not say that lightly. She is also a *bound soul*, trapped by a living witch, and I would not be a necromancer worth the name if I did not find that a problem that wants a solution. I have not yet decided whether the solution is to kill Verona, as the party assumes, or to learn how she bound Eva, or both.

Her warning about the libraries is why the Doctor's refusal to let me in the door matters so much more than I first thought.
