# Ubral
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Where:** [[church-of-st-andral]] (vigil) · **Toward us:** ally, wary

## What I know
A cleric of Helm and lord of Barovia's armed forces, held eleven days in Ravenloft's dungeon. He is at least partly undead, which his faith calls an abomination, and my divination confirms that Helm *still answers him*. I find the contradiction fascinating and also dangerous. If he fully understands it, the consequences could be severe.

## Timeline
- [[session-04/writeup|Session 4]]: found in the last cell of the Ravenloft dungeon, twenty years older than expected, wounds healed too fast for eleven days. Said: *"Perhaps Helm has not abandoned us after all."* Recognised Ismark; called him "tainted" yet the rightful burgomaster. Refused Aldric's healing. Was dressed in armour by Dagomir and improved at once. Warned of the Red Riders and of the vampire sorcerer. Cast a daylight spell that revealed the vargulf.
- [[session-05/writeup|Session 5]]: revived Kethri after the clot exploded; urged the party to Vallaki; asked to share a tent only with Iolas and Aldric.
- [[session-04/writeup|Session 4]]: freed. Backed away from Aldric's healing.
- [[session-05/writeup|Session 5]]: revived Kethri; "I will say no more".
- [[session-06_2026-10-29/writeup|Session 6]]: vouched for at the gates; entered a 3-day vigil; gave Aldric his Helm shield.
