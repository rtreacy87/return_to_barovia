# Mirel
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Where:** with me · **Toward us:** bound to me

## What I know
**Domain of study:** Arcane recall

Once a scholar. I guide Mirel through basic cantrips to learn whether magical intuition or spell memory can persist beyond death. Results are inconsistent but occasionally promising.

*Nature:* a positive-energy spirit I can resummon to animate a body. It enjoys taking damage and dying, since death does not exist on the Positive Energy Plane. A new spirit must be negotiated with; this one has died enough that it does not seek death immediately.
