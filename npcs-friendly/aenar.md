*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Where:** unknown · **Toward us:** has helped us; **heed the warning**

## What I know
Aenar **helped us in our first fight with [[erabus]]**. (The details of that fight are in a session I have not yet written up; `TODO(verify)`: which session and what exactly he did.) On that evidence alone he is a friend.

And yet [[madam-eva]], from her prison in the Tser Pool, told Iolas and Gwen: *"Do not trust him."* She believes he **may have helped release the ancient power the witch [[verona]] serves.**

I have turned this over several times. The two facts do not contradict one another, and that is exactly what troubles me. A man may fight beside us against a vampire lord and still have opened the door that the vampire lord came through. A man may even have done both for the same reason. **I propose to treat him as an ally whose motives I have not verified**, and not to give him anything I should not like to see used against us. The warning stands.
