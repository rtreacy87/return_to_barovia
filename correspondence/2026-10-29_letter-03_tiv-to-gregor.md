# To Gregor, Head Archivist of Candlekeep
**In-game date:** 13 Uktar, 1498 DR (est.) · **Elapsed:** +1 day since my last letter (Day 2 since arrival) · **Location:** Vallaki, the Blue Water Inn · Route: Tser Pool → Tser Falls → West Gate → Vallaki

Gregor,

Civilisation, at last, and with it, a fresh supply of excellent problems. I am in the happiest frame of mind I have enjoyed since we crossed the mist.

## Recap
We left the Tser Pool at dawn, the banshee of Madam Eva having told us the vampire lords serve a greater power and search the libraries of Vallaki for something hidden long ago in the crypts of Ravenloft. Iolas recovered a sword from the grave of a Vistani leader, Stanimir; it chose Gwynlian, pointing at her when set down, and its blows burn with radiance. A pleasing detail for a student of symbolic law.

On the road, a figure in a black cloak came walking toward us. I sent an Arcane Eye to look at it. It saw the Eye at once and unmade it. Then it spoke in all of our minds, in the voice we heard at the Amber Temple: Erabus. He said we could not hide and that he would add our flesh to his own. He gave chase, grew wings, and then vanished somewhere near Vallaki's gates, not before I had thrown Mold Earth behind us enough times to slow his ground pursuit.

Vallaki has changed beyond recognition, and I confess I am enchanted. Its walls bristle with ballistae, trebuchets and *cannons* (Gregor, *cannons*; I watched soldiers hold a wall against a vampire host with them, and I would give a great deal for an afternoon with their designer), built over seven or eight years by a man called only "the Doctor", who arrived from outside Barovia, won over Lady Wachter, and now commands the town's defences, its guard and, I am told, its **libraries**, which are no longer free to the public. A drunken old guard captain whispered that Lady Wachter may be afraid of him. The Doctor takes "volunteers" into his laboratory; some come out and serve; some do not.

Lord Ubral is in poor shape, though I am glad to report that he is still our ally. Kethri's dislike of him continues undiminished, and I find myself more curious about its source than about his symptoms. The church of Saint Andral has taken him in for a three-day vigil. My divination confirmed that he still draws power from Helm without any doubt. That sits uneasily beside his condition, which I have not forgotten.

Craig is well and has begun to read my handwriting. The others remain, as you know, resting. The sorcerer is still in my keeping. I banished what was clever in him at Ravenloft; what remains is a negative-energy spirit that loathes the material plane, and I shall exchange it for one of mine as soon as I have an hour to spare.

## Hypotheses
1. **Erabus follows us by scrying or divination, not by tracking.** He recognised my invisible Eye, which suggests true sight or a ritual sensor; and he found us exactly on the road, as if our position were known. I intend to test this rather than assume it.
2. **Someone in Vallaki makes positive-energy undead.** On our walk I used a second Arcane Eye on a cloaked group shadowing us. They were undead, animated by *positive* energy in the same structure as my own work, and fled the moment I noticed. Gregor, I could scarcely contain myself. Either a rival practitioner exists, or my research has been replicated, or someone has found a better method than mine, and in every case I intend to find them and ask them a thousand questions.
3. **The Doctor is the key to the library, and perhaps to what the vampires seek.** The same man controls access to the very place the vampires want.

I should add, with some injury, that I have been told I am "in no position to request an audience" with the Doctor, nor to see the library. A scholar of Candlekeep! I mean to approach him as one scholar does another, by trade. The wards of Candlekeep make it impossible to destroy the knowledge in the walls. A man who guards a library and fears a vampire siege might pay a great deal for such a thing.

## Research requests
1. Precedents for **scrying detection and countermeasures**: how a scrying sensor is revealed, which wards defeat it, and what else can follow a person across a country (blood, oath, curse, mist-sensitivity).
2. Any account of **positive-energy undead** other than mine: living-sworn dead, radiant revenants, undead created by clerics of life or light.
3. Any record of a **physician-engineer** from beyond the mists who built a fortified town: any "Doctor" connected to artifice, volunteers, or laboratory experiment.
4. Any word of **Edrin**. Any word at all.
5. Whether **Rod's book** has said anything more. Erabus can wear another man's face; please ask Rod something only he would know.


— Tivwick Bramblequill
Scholar of Candlekeep
Researcher in Applied Positive-Energy Necromancy
