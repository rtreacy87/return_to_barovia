12 Uktar, 1498 DR
Gregor,

A little more than a day has passed, and Barovia has become considerably more interesting.

Strahd’s former dominion now appears divided among three lords: Carstark, Erabus, and Usheron. Their relationship is unclear, but they are not one unified power. Carstark’s soldiers, for example, do not appear to serve Erabus.

When we first entered Barovia, we found an undead man imprisoned in a cage. We now believe him to be Ismark, likely the rightful heir to the burgomaster of Barovia. He had apparently been imprisoned by Ubral’s forces, who controlled the village.

Had.

When we returned, the entire village had been slaughtered and Ubral himself imprisoned. The evidence points toward a vampire army, which is fascinating because it makes poor strategic sense. Vampires require living populations. A village is not merely territory; it is food. Destroying one suggests deliberate political action, perhaps against Ubral as a rival.

Two matters require your research.

First: Erabus initially appeared wearing Rod’s face. Not resembling him. Wearing his face. Whatever he used was beyond Disguise Self. At minimum, he has seen Rod. At worst, he has captured him. Please investigate what magic, ritual, or vampiric ability could reproduce another person so completely, and whether blood, physical contact, or death is required.

Second: Ubral appears to be at least partially undead.

Normally I would find this extraordinarily exciting. Unfortunately, Ubral is a devout servant of Helm, and to him undeath is an abomination. The contradiction between his faith and his condition could become dangerous if he fully understands what has happened.

I will continue investigating the three lords, Ismark’s claim, and Ubral’s condition.

As for Rod's letters: your description concerns me, especially now that we know Erabus can wear another man’s face. I am no longer willing to assume every message written in Rod’s book is actually being written by Rod.


— Tivwick Bramblequill
Scholar of Candlekeep
Researcher in Applied Positive-Energy Necromancy
