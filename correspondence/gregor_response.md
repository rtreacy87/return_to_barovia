Tiv,

Your message troubles me more than I care to admit. But I am glad you are alive and are able to send these tidings. 

Rod has received a similar book to speak to me as well, and I received his first words this evening. So at least we know he lives.

Though, after reading what he has chosen to record, I am uncertain how comforting that knowledge should be.

The notes are… strange. Graphic, even. Far more intimate than any reasonable man would commit to parchment, and certainly more than I ever wished to know about the private affairs of a fellow monk.

He claims that he has found Ireena.

More troubling still, he writes that he has been spending a great deal of time with her at some manner of festival. According to him, the two of them are—how do you say it?—making up for lost time.

He provides considerably more detail.

I shall not.

Tiv, I worry for our friend’s sanity.

There is something feverish in the writing. At moments he sounds like the man we knew, and then, without warning, the words become obsessive, euphoric, almost desperate. Perhaps he has simply been alone too long. 

Or perhaps he truly has found Ireena, and that worries me most of all.

Keep the book close. Write down anything that changes.

And Tiv?

If the book begins writing back when you aren’t holding the quill, close it.

—Gregor
