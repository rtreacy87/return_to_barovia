## What I make of them
Our Vistani companion, and the one among us with the most to lose from Barovia. The mist handed her the **Mist** card although she has **lost the Vistani gift of walking it freely.** That cannot be accident. Either the mist means to explain why she lost it, or it means to give it back; I have not decided which concerns me more. If anyone among us can speak to the mist, I suspect it is she.

She is also tired. She grew up in Barovia, and says life there was *good, by Barovian standards*, from the time she was about eleven until very recently. She is, in her own words, overwhelmed, and she doubts she is fit to fill the place [[stanimir]] left. I think she is wrong, and I have said so, in my own way.

## Their story so far
- A Vistani, raised in Barovia, who has seen her camp burned and her people scattered. She recovered **Madam Eva's crystal ball** from the wreckage at the [[tser-pool]]; the guards in Vallaki regard it with some suspicion.
- Her relationship with [[stanimir]], the Vistani leader whose grave lies at the pool, is deep and unresolved. She has never found the commitment to speak to him again and fears she is letting him down. When Iolas brought her his sword, she asked him whether *he* wished to speak with Stanimir, and he declined; the sword then chose her.
- She wept at the Marionette vision, and said without hesitation that **we must go to [[krezk]] and help her people.**
