# Kethri
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Card (Tarokka):** Tempter

## Build
**Monk**. Hunted by the **Clenched Hand**. `TODO(verify)`: level and monastic tradition.

## What I make of them
The most efficient person I have ever watched dislike someone, and the person whose recap of Session 5 I trust above my own. She writes that if the Clenched Hand should follow her into Barovia, *their hunt for her would soon turn to an attempt to merely survive.* I believe her.

Her card is the **Tempter**: is she the tempter, or the tempted? Both imply catastrophe, from different directions. She distrusts [[ubral]] with a certainty that precedes anything he has done, and I intend to understand why. She has also, to my considerable surprise, taken a kind of fondness to me, and I am keeping notes on that too.

## Their story so far
- Not from this land. She says she has no stake in Barovia's past, but that "at least some of these people have earned my attention if not yet my trust."
- She dislikes *relenting*, and does it anyway, mostly for Iolas's sake. She calls it a bad habit.
- Flinches at loud noise: at the roar of the Tser Falls she "panicked" and watched behind us. `TODO(verify)`: this is only from the audio.
- At Ravenloft she wanted to finish the vampire sorcerer with her bare hands.
- Her distrust of Ubral: "This man will bring us trouble. I know it." She wanted to share his tent to watch him through the night; he refused.
