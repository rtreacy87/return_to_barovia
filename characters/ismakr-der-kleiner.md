# Ismakr der Kleiner
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Card (Tarokka):** (unknown)

## Build
Warrior; wielder of a **Vorpal Sword**. `TODO(verify)`: class and level.

## What I make of them
The rightful **Burgomaster of Barovia**, found caged and undead in the dark. A man who has already been killed once, resurrected, and had his skin made into a cloak. He asked Iolas to watch him and tell him if whatever brought him back has twisted him into something he should not be. I understand that request better than I would like, and I have not been able to bring myself to run on him the divinations that I could.

## Their story so far
- Imprisoned by Ubral's forces (*"Your people put me in a cage."*). Told by [[dagomir]] and confirmed by [[ubral]] that he is the rightful burgomaster. Ubral also called him "tainted".
- Captured by Strahd's agents the night of the church fire in Vallaki; later killed and brought back. The details are not complete.
- Sister: [[ireena]].
- Does not yet know the full account of his own past.

## Abilities and gear I have seen
- The **Vorpal Sword**, with which he took the vargulf's head and (nearly) finished [[vardak]].
- Strength enough to raise the Ravenloft portcullis, at the cost of an injury.
- Carried +1 half-plate, a +1 spear and a +1 greatsword that may be sold in Vallaki.
