# Aldric Duskmore
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Card (Tarokka):** Innocent

## Build
**Twilight cleric** of the Morninglord. `TODO(verify)`: level and spell list.

## What I make of them
Sincere, compassionate and almost painfully earnest. I have watched men turn the word *innocent* into an insult, and I have watched Aldric turn it back into a virtue by sheer persistence. His card asks a question I find impossible to leave alone: is his purpose to **keep** that innocence, or to **lose** it? I suspect Barovia has an opinion.

He is also, without any ambiguity, **Lord Ubral's biggest admirer**. I have seen younger scholars worship an old master with less shine in their eyes.

## Their story so far
- He knew [[ubral]] before the mist brought us here. Asked in the dungeon how he came to be with "this lot", he said only that it was a long story and that he was *glad to see him alive*. I have not yet had the long story. I mean to.
- Ubral himself says we need "someone pure of heart, such as a follower of the Morninglord." The first time Aldric stepped forward to heal him, **Ubral backed away.** Aldric did not let that wound his reverence.
- Aldric argued Ubral's case to Kethri at the Tser Pool, and took the first watch that night.

## Abilities and gear I have seen
- Twilight magic in the service of a god of dawn: a gentle, protective discipline. In Ravenloft's great hall he and Ubral lent their spells to the party as we fought the vargulf and [[vardak]].
- Carries Ubral's **+2 shield bearing the holy symbol of Helm**, entrusted to him at the church in Vallaki before the vigil. A priest of the Morninglord, wearing the sign of Helm. He accepted it with every sign of being honoured, and said that the Morninglord "seems chill" about it.
