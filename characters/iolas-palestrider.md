# Iolas Palestrider
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Card (Tarokka):** Artifact

## Build
**Fighter** with a little **wizard training**. `TODO(verify)`: subclass (Eldritch Knight?) and level.

## What I make of them
The one among us who knows this land. He fought here ten years ago in the company that ended Strahd's reign, and is remembered for it: innkeepers keep his table. He carries a sword apparently tied to Barovia, and I cannot yet tell whether his Artifact card means the **blade** or the **bearer**. I do not know which I would rather it were.

He is also, quietly, the person the rest of us look to for judgement. Kethri has said so, in writing.

## Their story so far
- A hero of the previous campaign. [[yuric]] greeted him as one of "the heroes that saved Barovia", kept his old table at the Blue Water, and had thought he had gone from these lands for good. [[franz-nilski]] remembers him fondly.
- He was present at the **first clash with Strahd**, outside the orphanage and the church of St. Andral as it burned, a fight that "didn't go well for us". He fled the coffin shop with [[ireena]] that night; [[ismakr-der-kleiner]] was captured by other agents. **Iolas carries guilt for this**, and with Ubral admits he bears some responsibility for what became of Ismark. He has not yet told Ismark who he is.
- A former traveling companion of [[ubral]]. He was the one who unlocked Ubral's manacles.
- Close to [[stanimir]]; he gave Iolas a sword once before. He dug up the sword wrapped in wax cloth at Stanimir's grave himself, and has a history with the Vistani I do not fully understand, though the weight of it is visible on him.

## Abilities and gear I have seen
- A fighter's training with a little of the wizard's craft; I have not yet seen him cast.
- A sword tied to Barovia, whose nature is the subject of my continuing worry.
- Knowledge of the roads: the Tser Falls, the West Gate, the road to Vallaki, and the Vallaki streets. He recognised the Doctor's tower and the damage to the town in an instant.
- Presents as "Atlas Talestrider" in the transcript, which I take to be Whisper's contribution.
- The party's diplomat: he vouched for Ubral at the gate and so got us in.
