# Tivwick Bramblequill
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

**Card (Tarokka):** Necromancer · **Order:** Scholar of Candlekeep · **Research:** Applied Positive-Energy Necromancy

## Who I am
I was educated in the great libraries of Candlekeep, where curiosity and the quiet threat of the gnomish **Bleaching** made inquiry a matter of survival, not merely scholarship. I apprenticed under Archmage [[halvoryn-vexis]], a brilliant and ambitious wizard whose work turned inward, then secretive, then silent. I began to notice a pattern: the greatest minds do not die. They *vanish*. One by one they pursue lichdom and retreat from the world they sought to understand.

Knowledge was not lost. It was **withheld.**

It became personal when my mentor Halvoryn and my closest friend, [[edrin-vale]], both chose the same path. When Halvoryn completed his transformation I was forced into a last confrontation, and I did not face him alone. I stood beside [[grathok-the-remembered]], an awakened troll uplifted by experimental magic who became a devout cleric of Kelemvor. Together we destroyed Halvoryn.

We did not agree on what that meant. To Grathok it was justice. To me it was proof of a deeper problem. Lichdom is not a solution; it is a *failure of design*. It preserves existence at the cost of purpose, connection and contribution. The mind endures and turns inward.

I refused that trade. I do not reject undeath; I intend to **redeem** it. Through positive energy, controlled animation and cognitive anchoring I seek a transformation that preserves awareness *and* curiosity, identity and ethical grounding. Some early experiments involved condemned individuals, testing whether intention and memory could persist. Those trials produced my present research subjects.

I developed a method to encode and transmit my findings across planar boundaries so that my work reaches Candlekeep even from the most isolated domains. That is what brought me to Barovia. This land is not merely cursed; it is **structured**. Souls are trapped, death is cyclical, and Strahd persisted beyond any final end. A system, however flawed, that anchors existence beyond death can be understood. What can be understood can be improved.

Now that Edrin walks the same road as Halvoryn, the work is no longer theoretical. If I succeed, immortality will stop meaning abandoning the world, and mean *remaining part of it.*

### The nature of the spirits (working notes)
- Positive-energy spirits **enjoy taking damage and dying**. On the Positive Energy Plane there is no such thing as death, so dying is merely a thrilling ride. Left to themselves they will seek it out.
- My established subjects have died enough times in my service that they **no longer instantly seek death**. A *new* spirit must be **negotiated with** before it is bound, to ensure it does not throw itself at the nearest blade.
- [[vardak]], the vampire sorcerer I converted, has had his intelligence banished and carries only a *negative*-energy spirit, which hates the material plane. I need only find the time to **swap it** for one of my positive-energy spirits.
