# The Dungeons of Ravenloft
*Being the fourth entry in the field record of Tivwick Bramblequill, Scholar of Candlekeep*

**In-game date:** 12 Uktar, 1498 DR (estimated, `TODO(verify)`) · **Elapsed:** Day 1 since arrival in Barovia · **Location:** [[castle-ravenloft]], the dungeons, the chapel and the great hall · Route: [[barovia-village]] → [[castle-ravenloft]] dungeon → chapel → great hall

> *Reconstructed from Iolas's written recap ([[session-04/player_recap_iolas]]). I have kept to what he recorded and added only what I saw or can reasonably infer. How we reached the dungeon is not in his account (`TODO(verify)`).*

## In brief
We stood in the dungeons of Castle Ravenloft, with one vampire and several ghoul-like creatures within arm's reach. I have been asked, in my time, to describe the most dangerous room I have ever stood in. I shall now have to revise the answer.

By nightfall we had found [[ubral]] alive in a cell, learned that three vampire lords have carved up Strahd's old domain, watched a good man die, been warned by a singing voice, and killed a vampire sorcerer's pet monster. I would call that a productive day.

## The Sleeping Vampire (the dungeon)
The ghouls did not move. The vampire, for its part, seemed to be wrapped in some kind of trance. A cautious party would have crept past. I will confess that I did not have the patience for caution when I had a *Haste* spell and a swordsman who had just discovered a **Vorpal Sword**.

I cast *Haste* on [[ismakr-der-kleiner]]. The result was a flurry of strokes from a weapon that removes heads, and the vampire was dispatched before it had the chance to move. Iolas, to whom I owe this account, called it "perhaps not the fairest of fights". We need make no apologies in this realm, and I do not.

The party spread out through the chamber. [[kethri]], who is more careful than any of us, examined the primary room and found **deep slash marks** in the floor, the sort made by something very large. At the time it was merely foreboding. Within the hour it was a *vargulf*.

## The Prisoner (the cell)
[[iolas-palestrider]] took the keys from the vampire's body and opened the door it had been guarding. The smell of blood, sweat and urine told us what lay at the end of the corridor before we had seen it: a man in the last cell, doused in blood and grime, in manacles. Iolas unlocked them. It was **Ubral**, looking twenty years older than anyone remembered.

I noted at once, as one does, that many of his wounds were *older and further healed* than eleven days could account for. Iolas noted it too. Is it some magic of Barovia's bond with him, or something darker? I make no guess in print. Yet.

His first words were for water. His second: *"You came. Perhaps Helm has not abandoned us after all."* We allowed him a few minutes' grace before asking what had happened here. He answered: *"The enemy possessed military precision and attacked with ruthless brutality. They sought something ancient and hidden but ultimately left empty-handed."*

His eyes settled on Ismark. *"I know what's left of your face,"* Ubral said. *"Your people put me in a cage,"* replied Ismark, evenly. Ubral seemed genuinely dismayed and asked how it had come to pass. Ismark gave what account he could; there are still gaps. [[dagomir]], Ubral's man, then told Ismark he is the **rightful Burgomaster of Barovia**. *"This is true,"* said Ubral. *"You come from an honored bloodline but you are tainted."*

Iolas told us he and Ubral share responsibility for what became of Ismark, and that he had avoided telling Ismark who he is. I record this because it is a promise: he means to. I intend to give him the quiet that it requires.

[[aldric-duskmore]] was nearly reverent in Ubral's presence. Ubral asked how Aldric had come to be with "this lot"; Aldric would say only that it was a long story and that he was glad to see him alive. Then Ubral said that we would need *someone pure of heart, such as a follower of the Morninglord*. Aldric stepped forward to heal him, and **Ubral backed away.**

Reader, I have seen a great many patients refuse a healer. None have refused one with such *speed.* Iolas thought he was hiding something. So did I. I also noticed Kethri had positioned herself further from him than anyone, and I filed that away too.

Dagomir took Ubral's armour from the wall and dressed him, and Ubral visibly improved. He knelt to pray. I do not share his practice, but there was something steadying in watching the one constant in this land go on being constant.

Looking at [[gynlian-glo]], he said: *"The Vistani rode west to the Summer Court. Some of my men followed them, but they never returned. We must reach Vallaki, even though House Watcher has no love for Helm."*

## The Tarokka
Gwen drew her cards. I watched Ubral grow uneasy but say nothing. Her eyes went white, and after some minutes she dropped the deck and told us what she had seen.

**The Artifact.** *A king with a ruined throne sits upon a longship under a black sky. He holds a sword, the artifact itself. It is darker than shadow, older even than the kingdom of which Barovia was once a part.* This, she said, is what the vampires seek.

**The Marionette.** *Music, laughter and firelight; Vistani dance around a bonfire. Among the revelers is a monk, though it is not Kethri or Rod. Banners bearing a blazing heraldic sun ripple above, but something is wrong. While the dancing continues the people cry, and a woman says "Help us." On the horizon lies Krezk and its monastery.*

Gwen wept. *"We must go to Krezk and help my people."*

We agreed that the artifact was likely what the vampires were seeking and that it may once have lain in Ravenloft. That the monk is neither Kethri nor Rod troubles me more than I intend to say. First we had to find our way out; Dagomir told us it was through the great hall.

## A Warning, and the Chapel
Before we left, Ubral spoke to Iolas: the main host of the **Red Riders** has departed, but some remain, and they are *far greater* than the foes we faced the last time Iolas was here. The captain of the garrison is a vampire sorcerer of considerable power, **Verik Morgain** (the name Iolas heard; I later learned him as [[vardak]]). *"Do not be surprised if we encounter him shortly."*

Dagomir led us to a pair of heavy double doors, the **chapel**. When he opened them a wall of stench hit us that dwarfed Ubral's cell: eleven days of death, though some corpses were far more recent. Some had been chewed and dragged. The scratches matched the claw marks downstairs.

Gwen cast *Speak with Dead* on one body and asked, *"What happened?"* The answer was *"A great slaughter. Other creatures emerged afterward, including a huge cat-like beast led by a sorcerer."* "Any other survivors?" *"None live."* "Any message?" *"None. Everyone I love is here."* Gwen apologised to Ubral at once. And then we heard singing.

## The Voice
A voice, which Ubral seemed to recognise, sang. When the song ended it spoke, echoing:

> *"Ubral, the Unbroken. Eleven days you endured. Impressive. Perhaps you endured because you changed."*

*"I do not fear death,"* said Ubral. *"That is why we give you life,"* said the voice. It told us that Ubral knew nothing of what they sought, that **its master, Lord Karstark, had ridden to Vallaki**, and that **Erabus** was not far from our trail.

Ubral answered with what I hope was real confidence: *"I do not know why you torment these lands, but they are no longer undefended."*

## Dagomir
Dagomir drew his sword. Dust fell from the rafters. Something took him from above, and we heard a wet, sickening crunch. Blood fell in a stream to the floor.

Ubral's spell shone like daylight, and in it we saw the thing: **a vargulf**, long-limbed and unnaturally tall, a vampiric cat, feasting on his body. Above us, rising fifteen feet off the ground, floated **Verik Morgain**.

I can give a clear account of my own part, and of little else; the rest was the usual blur. Ubral and Aldric lent their magic to the party. Gwen and I cooperated to trap the vargulf in a **wall of force**, with a sickening green radiance I conjured around it. Ismark, Kethri and Iolas struggled to get to our enemies until the sorcerer freed the beast. Then we turned on it, and Ismark took its head with the Vorpal Sword.

That left the sorcerer alone, and our numbers were more than he could handle. Several of us landed real blows, but it was Ismark again who ended it. Or nearly. We looked down. He had one ounce of life left.

## What I Learned
- Ubral lives but is not what he was, and he refused Aldric's healing. I want to know why.
- Ismark is the rightful Burgomaster. Ubral says he is also "tainted."
- Strahd's domain is held by three vampire lords: **Karstark**, **Erabus**, and a third, known so far only through the sorcerer's words.
- The vampires seek a sword (the Artifact) that may have been kept here.
- The Vistani are in danger at Krezk.
- Dagomir is dead.
