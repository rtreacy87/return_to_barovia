# The Road to Vallaki
*Being the sixth entry in the field record of Tivwick Bramblequill, Scholar of Candlekeep*

**In-game date:** 13 Uktar, 1498 DR (estimated) · **Elapsed:** +1 day since my last letter to Gregor (Day 2 since arrival in Barovia) · **Location:** [[vallaki]], the [[blue-water-inn]] · Route: [[tser-pool]] → [[tser-falls]] → [[west-gate-of-barovia]] → past the fork to the [[bonegrinder]] → Lake Zarovich road → [[vallaki]] → [[church-of-st-andral]] → [[blue-water-inn]]

> *Audio index: timestamps in parentheses point into `raw_transcript.txt`. The first seven minutes of the recording are Kethri's account of the previous session read aloud; I have not repeated them.*

## In brief
Reader, I have been chased down a haunted road by a vampire lord who speaks into one's mind, admitted to a fortress town through a wall of cannons, fed a wolf burger, and given a mystery involving undead very like my own. It was, by a comfortable margin, **the best day of the journey so far**, and I intend to record all of it.

A few things stood out. Lord Ubral, for all his troubling condition, is still our ally and still favoured by his god, and I am relieved beyond what I would say aloud. Erabus has a way of finding us that I do not yet understand, and I am *delighted* to have a puzzle worth the name. And the town's Doctor controls the one library I wish to enter, and has so far declined to let me.

## Morning at the Tser Pool (~00:07)
The camp rested properly. Iolas, Aldric and Kethri kept the watches; I did not, and I do not apologise, since the sleep of a wizard is a working part of his instrument.

Around three in the morning the watch heard screaming and explosions far off toward Vallaki. It lasted perhaps an hour. They judged it distant and not worth waking us for. I would have judged it *data*. I shall say so, politely, later.

[[iolas-palestrider]] gave [[gynlian-glo]] the short sword he had dug from [[stanimir]]'s grave. She hesitated, and then did something I found rather moving: she asked whether Iolas wished to speak with Stanimir while a Speaker of the Dead was present. The conversation turned toward what Stanimir might have wanted of her, and whether she was fit to fill his place. Iolas gently told her what he thought. The question of disinterring the corpse was raised and, to my professional regret, declined. The sword settled it by itself. Set down on the ground, it turned and pointed at Gwen. It is a **+2 short sword that deals radiant damage**. The weapon chose its bearer. I note that this is *symbolic law* again. She has no training with it, but the blade appears to consider itself family. The DM allows her proficiency, "through the bond".

Before we left I confirmed the one thing I had spent a spell slot on the night before. I asked, with my *Divination*: *Does Ubral take commands and gain power from the deity he claims?* The answer was **yes, without uncertainty**. His power from Helm is real. A man partly undead, still in favour with his god, and the god does not mind. I confess that I was relieved. For a day I had worried that the god might have abandoned a man who still kneels to him. He has not. Ubral remains an ally, a problem of the best sort. Kethri, by contrast, remains his harshest critic, and I find that the more interesting question. She was cold toward him before he did anything to deserve it, so what, I wonder, does she know that I do not? I mean to ask, tactfully, and then less tactfully.

I summoned a **Phantom Steed** for Ubral, who cannot yet walk well.

## The road west (~00:32)
We passed the Tser Falls, where Iolas remembered a revenant of a silver dragon that once guarded the old stone bridge. It is gone. The water roars so loudly that nothing else can be heard, which is *why* I noticed what I did not hear. When we came out of the noise, the forest was silent. No birds, no insects. Nothing. A good eye among us saw the road torn up by recent traffic: foot, horse, no wagons, all moving toward Vallaki.

At the **West Gate**, with its two headless sentinels, Gwen was reluctant to commune with her patron through one of the heads, having been pulled into something last time at the eastern gate. She tried anyway, in a careful way, with a roll of 24 on Arcana, and **felt no presence whatever.** I record this as a datum and an unease. (`TODO(verify)`: whether it was Gwen.)

At the fork to the **Bonegrinder** my Arcana check struck me in the stomach. The burned windmill gives off hag-magic of a kind I have only read about, and it hurt. I wished to investigate. I was outvoted, gently, and for good reason. Ubral could not afford a detour. *I shall return.*

Closer to the lake the tracks changed: warhorses, something larger and monstrous, a host of undead. Much like the vargulf. No Vistani sign.

## Erabus (~00:53)
Visibility cleared enough to see three hundred yards in either direction. Behind us, in the middle of the road, stood a single black-cloaked figure with no face.

I sent the Arcane Eye back to look at it. **It saw the Eye at once.** The Eye is invisible; it should not have been seen. The figure turned toward it, and the Eye simply ceased. Not a dispel; a *death*. Whatever it is, it perceives through invisibility, or through the spell itself.

I will admit something here that I would not put in a letter to Gregor: my first sensation was not fear. It was *thrill*. I was looked at, through my own spell, by something that wished to kill me, and the only thought in my head was *how?* A man does not travel to the end of the known world to avoid being surprised by it.

Then every one of us heard a voice in our minds, a voice we last heard in the Amber Temple:

> *You thought you could hide. We are on the main road… I will add your quivering flesh to my own.*

**Erabus.** Huge arms unfolded from the figure and it began to run, at speed, toward us.

Discussion was brief and, for once, sensible: run for Vallaki, put the walls at our backs, and hope that the town guard was a friend or at worst a distraction. We kicked the Steeds into a gallop. I cast **Mold Earth** behind us, again and again, laying difficult terrain across the road like a scholar scattering books in a pursuer's path. Whatever it was had to slow. Then it sprouted wings and rose into the air. Someone placed *Protection from Evil and Good* on Ubral (Aldric, I believe), and he began to seize.

We rounded the bend. When I next looked back, **Erabus was gone**. Not a trace on the road.

*The question that has taken over my mind:* **how is he finding us?** He appeared on the road behind us at precisely the right time. He knew we were on the main road, and said so. If it is divination, it can be detected and perhaps defeated; if it is something else, a blood link, a creature, an oath, then I need to know that too. I have a list of tests and I am quite eager to begin.

*Observation:* he chased until it became pointless and then stopped. He did not attack the walls. He did not wait. It looked less like a hunter who lost his quarry than one who had **made his point**.

## The Walls of Vallaki (~01:08)
The approach is a field of the dead: guardsmen, undead, fallen warhorses, and the ash of what I take to be vampires. A ballista fired a warning bolt a few feet in front of us. I took Ubral's holy symbol and held it aloft, lit, so that the watchers could see what rode with us. Iolas dismounted. We were told to advance slowly.

I should record what Iolas saw that I could not know: **Vallaki has become a fortress.** Ballistae, trebuchets, and something new, *cannons*, were built into the ramparts, far beyond anything he remembered. I have read of black powder; I had not expected to see it fired in a domain of vampires. Someone in this town is *inventing*, and I would very much like to see how. Two banners hang outside: Lady Wachter's watching eye, and a new geometric sigil in the shape of a cannon. The second is not hers.

A captain with a plumed helm (**Captain Draska**, if I heard it right) demanded our purpose. Iolas named himself and Ubral. The captain's voice changed when he saw Ubral. He knew the name. He also told us, with some pride, that they had held off a horde of zombies and larger undead warriors **that very morning** with the Doctor's siege weapons. Ubral, to his credit, warned him in no uncertain terms that what the captain had faced was only a vanguard.

I watched the faces on the ramparts as they cheered. These people stood on a wall against zombies and a vampire host and **held it**. Their machines work. I cannot decide whether this is the most reassuring thing I have seen in Barovia or the most suspicious, and I am wildly curious about the mechanisms. If anyone has a notebook, I would like to borrow it.

We were admitted on Ubral's authority, though he is, we were told, "no friend of Lady Wachter". A corporal, **Bestwick**, was assigned to guide us, and also to watch us. He searched us with great care. He took a keen interest in Gwen's crystal ball, taken from Madam Eva's caravan, and in the fact that she travels with us at all, since the Vistani left through this gate "twelve nights ago".

I had my own undead hold back at the gate. A crowd of zombies at a fortress door is how a negotiation ends.

## Vallaki, the Living Town (~01:27)
For the first time since we arrived, there are *people*. Merchants, smiths, an alchemist, a town hall with a stage under a tarp, the old coffin shop, and the **tower laboratory** in the square which Gwen and Iolas remember. The air smells of smoke and bread.

At the **Blue Water Inn** [[yuric]] greeted Iolas as a returning hero. He had a table kept for us, and wolf burgers, and wine from the Wizards of Wine. I will say only that I have not eaten like this in a decade and that I do not apologise for the second helping. The wolf burger was *excellent*, a gloriously uncivilised thing, though I thought it carried rather too much garlic. Then I remembered what town I was in and what has been at its gates, and I decided the garlic was a reasonable precaution, and finished it.

At the bar sat a drunk, one-armed, one-eyed man: [[franz-nilski]], formerly the captain of the guard. He greeted Ubral by name.

## Ubral's Vigil (~01:42)
Iolas and the others took Ubral to the **Church of Saint Andral**, rebuilt since the fire. The priest, **Father Alexi Voss** (as best I can tell), sensed the darkness in Ubral the moment he arrived. He prescribed a **three-day vigil**: armour on the altar, forehead to the stone, awake in the presence of the saint's bones. If it works, Ubral is cleansed and the saint himself may speak to him. If it does not: *this may be farewell.* Ubral accepted. Before going in, he pressed his **+2 shield bearing Helm's holy symbol** into Aldric's hands, a priest of the Morninglord, to carry. I would pay a great deal to understand what passed between them.

The vigil should end on or about **16 Uktar**.

## The Watchers (~02:11)
On the way back to the inn, Iolas (or one of the others) noticed that we were being followed by a group of cloaked figures. I cast **Arcane Eye** on them.

They are not men. They are **undead, and they are animated by positive energy**, with the same structure I use in my own work. They do not hold the shape of the dead as Craig does; they hold it the way *my subjects* do, the way I built it. The instant I examined them, they dissolved into the crowd.

I have been working on this problem for years, and **I am overjoyed**. There are *more* of them. Someone, somewhere in Vallaki, has found the same method, or taken mine, or the mist has handed it to them, and any of those is a research lead worth a month of my life. I want to talk to them, study them, and learn how they are made. I did, regrettably, startle them, and I shall do better next time. The guard knows nothing of them; the corporal is a living man.

## Over Wolf Burgers (~02:17)
Franz, drunk but plainly honest, and Yuric, careful and kind, gave us the lay of the land. I pressed for the dates.

- **The Doctor** arrived seven or eight years ago from beyond the borders of Barovia, "perhaps during the weakening of the walls". He persuaded Lady Wachter to fund his tower and his laboratory. He designed the defences. The town adores him. Franz says, quietly, that he has more power than Lady Wachter and that *she is afraid of him*.
- **The libraries are no longer free.** They are under the Doctor's control. Access requires an audience with Lady Wachter or the Doctor.
- The fortifications predate the vampires. The vampires have been here fourteen days. The walls have been built for seven or eight years. *Against what, Franz could not say.*
- The Doctor holds **public demonstrations every two weeks**, impromptu, on the festival stage. The next is unannounced.
- He recruits **volunteers**. "Some emerge. Some don't." Those who do are rather like the troops Franz once trained. Captain Draska was one. The old guard, and Franz with it, has been replaced.
- The Vistani left a fortnight ago to the **northwest**, in a great caravan.
- Yuric has heard nothing from the **Wizards of Wine** in over two weeks. He asked us to look into it.
- When I asked whether the guard had any knowledge of the positive-energy undead, they did not.

The Doctor builds machines I would give a year of my life to understand, and holds a library I was told, in so many words, that **I am in no position to request an audience for**. A scholar from Candlekeep. I will confess to being a little insulted. I will also confess that I understand it: he does not know me, and I am wearing a travelling cloak and standing next to a partly undead cleric. I intend to correct that. The Doctor fills his laboratory with volunteers who come out changed, and so far as I am concerned this is a reason to *go in*, politely.

## Close of the Day (~02:40)
We settled our affairs. I wish to identify the **staff** that came from the vampire sorcerer and have not yet. The shop appendix and the staff's description will be sent between sessions. We are short on coin but not entirely: we have ten gems worth a hundred gold each, three healing potions, and a quantity of +1 equipment (half-plate, spear, greatsword) which looks too much like vampire gear to sell without risk.

Kethri and Ismark will walk past the library with me. I intend to read its wards.

We are in a safe place. I am told that is rare. The previous version of me would have asked to see the Doctor's laboratory before the soup was cold, and I am now only a little behind.

## What I Learned (Summary)
- Ubral still draws power from Helm; his vigil ends around **16 Uktar**.
- Erabus sees through invisibility, speaks into our minds, and found us on the road. He vanished near Vallaki's walls.
- Positive-energy undead walk Vallaki. I did not make them.
- The Doctor holds the keys to the library the vampires seek.
- Vallaki is a fortress for a siege that began *before* the vampires arrived.
