# Tiv's Private Journal — Session 6
**In-game date:** 13 Uktar, 1498 DR (est.) · **Elapsed:** Day 2 since arrival · **Location:** Vallaki, Blue Water Inn · Route: Tser Pool → Tser Falls → West Gate → Vallaki

## Chapter Six: The Road to Vallaki
*From the book I am writing, working title:* **A Scholar in the Mist**

The morning came up grey over the Tser Pool, as mornings do in Barovia, which is to say that it was not so much a dawn as a slow admission that the night had ended. We broke camp quietly. Somewhere beneath the pool an old woman who had once been Madam Eva wailed to herself, and I confess that I looked back at the water more than once.

It was on the road beyond the falls that I first understood the shape of the day. The water roared so loudly that nothing else could be heard, and when we came out from the noise the forest was *silent*, the kind of silence that has a listener in it. I sent my Arcane Eye ahead, as a careful man does, and then behind us, as a *very* careful man does, and it was the second decision that made this chapter worth writing.

On the road stood a figure in a black cloak with no face at all. It saw my Eye. I want the reader to understand how strange that is, because the Eye is invisible and no one is meant to see it. The figure turned its head toward the place where my spell hung in the air, and the spell ended. I have been struck, burned and frozen by a great many things, but I had never before been *looked at* through my own magic, by something that knew exactly what it was looking at and exactly what to do about it.

And then the voice, in every mind at once, the one we had heard in the Amber Temple. *You thought you could hide.* Reader, I will tell you what I felt, because honesty is the price of a good account: a cold from the scalp to the heels, and beneath it something much warmer and much more dangerous, which was *joy*. I have spent a lifetime reading the accounts of those who walked into peril and wrote it down afterward, and I envied every one of them. Now a vampire lord was running us down a haunted road, and every part of me was crying *this is going to be a magnificent chapter*. I think there is something wrong with me. I would not change it.

We ran. I laid difficult earth behind us like a man scattering books in a pursuer's path. The thing grew wings and rose, and I thought of nothing but the question that has not left me since: **how does he find us?** He stood behind us on the main road at the right moment. He told us we could not hide. If it is divination, I can test it. If it is a creature, a mark, a bond, a promise, I shall learn which. I have not been so excited by a problem since my first successful animation.

Then, as quickly as he had come, he was gone, and before us stood Vallaki.

I must describe Vallaki, because it is the first place in all of Barovia that has surprised me in a good way. The walls bristled with ballistae and trebuchets and, I swear on every volume in Candlekeep, *cannons*. The field before the gate was strewn with the dead of a battle fought that very morning, and on the ramparts the guards were cheering. They had stood against zombies and a vampire host and **held**. Their machines work. I cannot tell you whether that is the most reassuring thing I have seen here or the most suspicious, but I can tell you I wanted to take every one of them apart to see how.

We were fed. Let history record it. After days of rations and dread, [[yuric]] set a wolf burger in front of me at the Blue Water Inn, and it was a gloriously uncivilised thing, and I thought it contained rather too much garlic. Then I remembered what town I was in and what had been at its gates, and I decided the garlic was a perfectly reasonable precaution, and finished it. I had a second.

There is a gentleman called the Doctor who holds the library of Vallaki, and who, I am told, controls its doors. I asked to see him and was informed that I was *in no position to request an audience.* A Scholar of Candlekeep. I shall be generous and assume he has not heard of us. He will.

Lord Ubral is for now in the hands of the saint. When my divination answered *yes, without uncertainty*, I sat down. He is partly dead and his god still hears him; I do not know what that means, but I know I am glad he is ours. Kethri, who dislikes him with an intensity I cannot trace to anything he has done, watches him as though he were a lit fuse. Why? That is a thread, and I do mean to pull it.

And on the way home, as the afternoon thickened, I cast one more Eye at the cloaked shapes that had been following us through the crowd, and they were *undead*, reader. Animated by **positive energy**, in a structure I know as well as my own hand. More of them. I think I laughed. I know I startled them, and they melted into the street, but I have the shape of them now and I mean to find the hand that made them.

## Ideas
- The voice in my head reached all of us at once. If Erabus holds some link to the party, it may be what he follows. I should ask each of them whether they still feel anything.
- Ubral is half undead; Erabus is a vampire lord. Could Ubral be a beacon? I do not like the thought. I should test it anyway.
- The forest by the falls was silent. No birds. Is Erabus watching through animals, or did the animals simply leave?
- The cloaked watchers: the Doctor's volunteers who did not return? A rival necromancer? Or, and this is the one I like least and want most, something the mist produced in answer to my research.
- A Candlekeep ward as barter for the Doctor's library. A man who holds a library against a siege should value one that cannot be destroyed.

## Experiments to Run

### Is Erabus tracking us by divination?
- **What I think:** He follows us by scrying or divination, or a sensor on one of us or one of our things.
- **How I shall test it:** (1) *Detect Magic* on every one of us and every possession: the crystal ball, Stanimir's sword, Vardak's staff, Ubral's gear. (2) *Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum* on the inn room, and see whether he seems to lose us. (3) Two small groups, two different false itineraries; see where he turns up. (4) A decoy under *Nondetection* on the road.
- **If I am right:** he finds us only when we are unshielded, or at a leaked destination.
- **If I am wrong:** it is something physical or bound to a creature, and I turn to birds, wolves and bloodlines.
- **What I need:** spell slots, Gwen's help with her crystal ball, and the willingness of my companions to lie convincingly.

### The watching undead of Vallaki
- **What I think:** They are animated by the same positive-energy structure as mine, and their maker is here.
- **How:** Do not chase them. An *Alarm* by the inn door, an Arcane Eye on the street, and a pulse of my own signature to see whether anything answers. If one is ever destroyed, *Speak with Dead* is a possibility.
- **What I need:** a quiet corner, Kethri and Ismark nearby, and nerve. I have the first two and abundance of the third.

### The library's wards
- A walk past with *Detect Magic*, as a ritual, to read what protects the place. I shall find a way into the library, and I shall find a way to read its books. I have no doubt of that.
- Kethri and Ismark will come with me. Kethri, I suspect, because she does not wish me to be eaten; Ismark, because he is polite.

### Vardak's spirit
Very simple. I banished the clever part of him at Ravenloft, and the negative-energy spirit that remains hates the material plane and has no opinions worth recording. It wants out; I have a replacement. All I need is a little time and a quiet room. I think I shall use Bram: reliable, consistent, and unlikely to try dying on the first night.
