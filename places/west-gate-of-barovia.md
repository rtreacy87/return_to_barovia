# West Gate of Barovia
*From the field notes of Tivwick Bramblequill.*

## What I saw
Two headless sentinels. The heads may lie under the moss-covered mounds nearby.

## Why it matters
Gwen reached for her earth djinn patron here and felt no presence. At the eastern gate she had been pulled in.
